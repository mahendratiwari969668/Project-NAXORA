# Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Overview

### Product Name

**TBD**

### Product Type

Web-based platform for **Academia–Industry collaboration, skill mapping, internships and placement**.

### Product Vision

Build a single platform that connects **students, colleges/universities and companies** so that student skills can be mapped against real industry requirements, skill gaps can be identified, and relevant internship and placement opportunities can be discovered and managed.

The platform should act as a bridge between:

**Student Skills → College Development → Industry Requirements → Opportunities → Hiring**

---

# 2. Problem Statement

There is often a gap between what students learn in academic institutions and the skills companies actually require.

### Students face problems such as:

* Not knowing which skills are required for a particular career.
* Difficulty identifying their actual skill gaps.
* Finding relevant internships and entry-level opportunities.
* Managing resumes, projects, applications and opportunities across different platforms.
* Not knowing whether their current skill set matches a particular opportunity.

### Colleges face problems such as:

* Limited visibility into the skill distribution of their students.
* Difficulty identifying common skill gaps.
* Difficulty connecting students with relevant industry opportunities.
* Managing internship and placement-related information.
* Lack of structured industry skill-demand data.

### Companies face problems such as:

* Finding candidates with relevant skills.
* Filtering large numbers of applications.
* Communicating required skills clearly.
* Finding suitable students for internships and entry-level roles.

---

# 3. Product Goals

The platform should:

1. Connect students, colleges and companies.
2. Create structured student skill profiles.
3. Map student skills against industry requirements.
4. Identify skill gaps.
5. Help students discover relevant internships and placement opportunities.
6. Help companies discover candidates based on skills.
7. Help colleges understand student skill trends and gaps.
8. Provide AI-assisted resume and skill analysis.
9. Provide a centralized application and opportunity tracking system.
10. Provide role-specific dashboards.

---

# 4. Target Users

The platform will have three primary user types.

## 4.1 Students

Students looking for:

* Skill development
* Internships
* Projects
* Entry-level jobs
* Placement opportunities
* Career guidance

## 4.2 Colleges / Universities

Institutions that want to:

* Manage students
* Understand skill distribution
* Identify skill gaps
* Track internships
* Monitor placement readiness
* Connect with companies

## 4.3 Companies

Companies that want to:

* Post internships
* Post jobs
* Define required skills
* Discover candidates
* Shortlist candidates
* Manage applications
* Connect with colleges

---

# 5. Public Landing Page

The landing page will be the primary public entry point of the platform.

It should clearly communicate:

* What the platform does.
* Who it is for.
* What problem it solves.
* How the platform works.
* Core capabilities.
* How students benefit.
* How colleges benefit.
* How companies benefit.

## Landing Page Structure

### 5.1 Navigation

Navigation should contain:

* Brand / Logo
* Platform
* For Students
* For Colleges
* For Companies
* About
* Login
* Register

Login and Register should not immediately open a role-specific form.

They should first open a **role selection interface**.

---

### 5.2 Hero Section

The hero section should communicate the product's core value clearly.

Example direction:

> **Connect skills with real opportunities.**

Supporting message:

> A platform connecting students, colleges and industry to map skills, discover opportunities and build stronger pathways from education to employment.

Primary CTA:

**Get Started**

Secondary CTA:

**Explore Platform**

The hero should use an actual product UI preview/dashboard visualization rather than generic AI-generated people or stock imagery.

---

### 5.3 Problem Section

Explain the gap between:

**Student Skills**

and

**Industry Requirements**

The section should establish why the platform is necessary.

---

### 5.4 Three User Types

Introduce the three platform participants:

#### Students

Build profiles, map skills, identify gaps and discover opportunities.

#### Colleges

Understand student skill distribution, identify training needs and monitor internship and placement activity.

#### Companies

Define skill requirements, discover relevant candidates and manage internship and hiring pipelines.

---

### 5.5 How It Works

The landing page should explain the core workflow:

1. Build Profile
2. Map Skills
3. Discover Opportunities
4. Connect and Apply
5. Track Progress

---

### 5.6 Skill Mapping Section

Demonstrate how student skills can be compared with a target role.

Example:

**Student Skills**

* JavaScript
* React
* Node.js
* MongoDB

**Target Role**

Full Stack Developer

**Potential Skill Gaps**

* TypeScript
* Docker
* Automated Testing

This section is a product demonstration, not a claim about real users.

---

### 5.7 Internship & Placement Section

Show how students can discover opportunities based on their profile and skills.

Opportunity information may include:

* Role
* Company
* Location / Work Mode
* Required Skills
* Eligibility
* Application deadline
* Skill match information
* Application status

---

### 5.8 AI Assistance Section

AI will assist users with:

* Resume information extraction
* Skill extraction
* Skill-gap analysis
* Opportunity matching
* Learning recommendations
* Profile improvement suggestions

AI recommendations must be presented as **assistance**, not guaranteed outcomes.

---

### 5.9 College Section

Show how institutions can use the platform to understand:

* Student skill distribution
* Skill gaps
* Training requirements
* Internship participation
* Placement-related activity
* Industry demand

---

### 5.10 Company Section

Show how companies can:

* Create opportunities
* Define required skills
* Discover candidates
* Filter candidates
* Review profiles
* Shortlist candidates
* Manage applications

---

### 5.11 Final CTA

The landing page should end with a clear call to action for the three user groups.

Examples:

* **Create Student Account**
* **Register Institution**
* **Register Company**
